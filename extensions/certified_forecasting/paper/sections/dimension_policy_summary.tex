\subsection{Local dimension selection and certificate utility}
\label{sec:dimension-policy}

Local PCA dimension affects the reconstruction map and its induced decoder
class. A separate study compares fixed cap two, native cap twenty, and
validation selection over $\{1,2,4,8,12,20\}$ under the block-direct decoder.
It uses \DimRepetitions{} fresh fitting repetitions on the same six synthetic
laws: five for each of the four reference laws and three per oscillator, with
128/512/4096/2048 training/validation/calibration/test episodes. Inputs are
training-standardized; histories, horizons, reference limits, $\kappa=4$,
and decoder ridge $10^{-4}$ follow the reference protocol.

Each policy selects one history--cap bank by its best prefix's mean validation
MSE over horizons, then freezes all four prefixes of that bank. This preserves
their nested classes. RBF and affine-hull controls retain their original
definitions; autoregressive, quadratic, persistence, and constant forecasts
complete the comparison. All \DimDevelopmentFits{} development fits finish,
and every retained model and validation choice is sealed, before any
calibration episode is generated.
The \DimEvaluatedModels{} evaluated model instances yield \DimRiskTails{}
risk endpoints and \DimImprovementTails{} planned improvement endpoints,
covered jointly by one failure probability $0.05$. Test generation follows
the complete calibration seal. Supplement Section~S9 gives the full protocol
and allocation; this event concerns the dimension study alone.

Validation-selected cap reduces mean test MSE on five laws and reproduces
cap two on quasiperiodic data (Table~\ref{tab:dimension-policy-summary}).
Across repetitions, it improves in \DimBetterRuns{}, ties in \DimEqualRuns{},
and worsens in none. It selects cap one, two, or four in
\DimCapOneCount{}, \DimCapTwoCount{}, and \DimCapFourCount{} repetitions,
respectively. All \DimSelectedForecasts{} selected KAHM forecasts use 30
retained coordinates. Native cap twenty has mixed effects, including a
\DimNativeDuffingIncrease\% increase in mean Duffing MSE. Its realized local
ranks are three, four, or twelve. Selected KAHM has lower mean MSE than the
non-KAHM comparators on nonlinear AR and logistic; quadratic regression
remains stronger on the other four laws.

The forecasting gains do not change the selected-forecast tolerance decisions.
For every policy, \DimAcceptLow{}, \DimAcceptMedium{}, and \DimAcceptHigh{}
of the 26 selected forecasts are accepted at RMSE tolerances $0.2$, $0.35$,
and $0.5$; all remaining decisions are unresolved. Decisions agree across
policies for every repetition and tolerance, and all
\DimSelectedLowerZero{} selected-KAHM horizon lower endpoints are zero.
There are \DimPositivePrefix{} positive improvement lower endpoints among
\DimPrefixTails{} adjacent-prefix comparisons, but none among the
\DimPolicyTails{} selected-policy comparisons. Thus dimension selection
improves descriptive forecasting accuracy here without establishing a
simultaneously certified policy improvement or increased selected-forecast
decision resolution.
