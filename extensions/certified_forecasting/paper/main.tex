\begin{abstract}%
Soft geometric state abstractions compress a time-series history into a small
collection of association weights, but neither geometric reconstruction nor a
small coordinate-prediction residual establishes accurate forecasting of the
observed signal. We develop a learning and certification procedure whose target
is the original future output. A pairwise discrepancy between target separation
and representation separation lower-bounds the risk of every decoder with a
prescribed Lipschitz budget. The bound is exact on a two-atom model and dominates
a root-variance obstruction when both use the same conditional pairing law.
Retaining geometric feature blocks and constraining the sum of their coefficient
norms gives nested forecasting classes without improving their apparent
capacity through exact feature replication. We also quantify a necessary amount
of geometric change for a refinement to meet a desired accuracy. Independent
episode averages yield simultaneous lower certificates for the prediction class,
upper certificates for fitted multihorizon forecasts, and certificates of actual
improvement between fixed refinements. Observations within episodes may be
arbitrarily dependent. We implement the method using Kernel Affine Hull Machine
coordinates, convex constrained decoders, and explicit abstention when a risk
requirement cannot be certified. Experiments evaluate exact-risk calibration,
four bounded episode laws, two nonlinear oscillators, and raw pen trajectories with writer-disjoint
evaluation. They distinguish valid but uninformative certificates from useful
decisions, and do not establish universal forecasting superiority or guarantees
for an arbitrary single dependent series. A training-only input-scaling
follow-up reproduces an accuracy gain on the fixed pen-trajectory cohort and
also records regressions.
\end{abstract}

\section*{Reproducibility and research provenance}
The implementation, frozen configurations, retained result summaries, and
figure generators are distributed in the
\path{extensions/certified_forecasting} directory of the accompanying
repository. Editable manuscript source is included in its \path{paper}
directory. The original results and the input-scaling follow-up are retained
separately, with frozen configurations, source hashes, and documented exact
replays of damaged score archives. Full per-episode archives are regenerated
by the fixed-seed runners and retained with the experimental evidence. The experimental
configurations and independent sampling units are specified in the appendices. No external journal submission is represented by
this draft. AI assistance was used for derivation exploration, implementation,
literature retrieval, and writing. Independent author verification of the
scientific claims, authorship information, and submission declarations is still
required; funding and conflict-of-interest declarations must be supplied by the
authors before submission.
