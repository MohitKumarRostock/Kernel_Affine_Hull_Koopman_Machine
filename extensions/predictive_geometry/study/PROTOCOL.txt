KAHM GEOMETRY MECHANISM STUDY, VERSION 1.0

Purpose
Identify whether KAHM reconstruction, score encoding, or retention of local
position improves prediction, conditional on a specified decoder and data law.
This study does not reproduce the manuscript's certification experiment and
does not estimate the optimal possible decoder risk from finite fitted models.

Source and implementation
The original KAHM autoencoder and kernel routines are pinned to repository
commit d6d74d1953a4310b73065a556df816e5ab5e3ebc. Every vendored file was checked
against its Git blob SHA-1 and has a recorded SHA-256. Upstream float32 reference
conversion and the exact-zero inner-normalizer convention are preserved.

All matched reconstructions share training-only input scaling, a maximum1600
training-row geometry subsample, KMeans partitions (2,4,8 clusters), at most24
references per cluster, local PCA basis and effective rank, folding score, and
affinity transform. Rank is at most2 and at most input dimension minus1.
KAHM uses its original nonlinear kernel reconstruction. Affine uses orthogonal
projection onto the same local basis through the same reference mean. A
centroid reconstruction is a simpler control. No primary partition uses S or
the outcome. The native score controls reuse this shared reference geometry;
they are not separately fitted reproductions of original native pipelines.

Outer affinity weights for each block are
  w_c=(1-T_c+tau)^omega, phi_c=w_c/sum_j w_j,
where omega16, tau1e-6 and T is the same folding score for all geometries.
Features include phi, phi plus one log(sum w) coordinate per block, complete
scores, radial/angle components, phi plus standardized raw inputs, and phi plus
gated local PCA coordinates phi_c*((z-center_c)@basis_c). The log-normalizer
is the OUTER group-affinity normalization, not the signed kernel-reference
normalizer inside KAHM reconstruction. phi+logZ is equivalent in information
to scores, but not in linear expressivity or regularization.

Data
Primary forecasting settings: nonlinear AR, Van der Pol, Duffing; exact pinned
generator laws, histories12, horizons1/4/12, episode length72, origin stride4,
13 origins per episode,128 training,128 validation,512 test episodes.
All origins of an episode remain in one split. Evaluation first averages
squared losses within episode and across horizons, then across episodes.
The RK4 generators are numerical laws, not exact continuous-time trajectories.

Primary branch settings: curvature c0/1 crossed with lambda0/.5/1, train1024,
validation512, test2048 independent points. S is equiprobable +/-1,
T uniform[-1,1], E uniform[-.1,.1], independent.
  X=(T,c*cos(pi*T)+.5*S)
  m=sqrt(1-lambda)*S+sqrt(3*lambda)*T; Y=m+E.
Signal variance1 and Bayes MSE1/300 are constant. X includes T, and S is
recoverable from X; there is no input noise or unavoidable regime ambiguity.
At each c and repetition the same fitted unsupervised bank and exactly the
same train/validation/test X and noise E are reused for all lambda. Across c,
latent samples are identical. A training-size256 nested subset is secondary.
No branch symmetry of a learned finite bank is assumed. Reflected-input and
nearest-neighbor diagnostics are empirical descriptions, not information-loss
certificates. Their scales are estimated using training features only.

Airfoil Self-Noise is a secondary static-regression check. Its fixed seed
2026103007 split holds out entire groups defined by the four nonfrequency
operating settings:64train/21validation/21test groups (905/295/303rows).
Twenty-four fitting seeds reuse that one dataset/split; they are not24 new
natural-data replications. Validation/test losses weight groups equally.
No population-wide outcome bound is claimed; predictions are not clipped.
Data provenance, license and exact group IDs are in DATA_NOTE.txt.

Heads and selection
All head standardization is training-only. Ridge minimizes mean squared loss
plus alpha times squared coefficient norm with unpenalized intercept. Alpha
grid1e-8,1e-6,1e-4,.001,.01,.1,1. The flexible diagnostic is a64-landmark
Nystrom Gaussian RBF plus a linear component, bandwidth multiplier.25/1/4
relative to the training-landmark median squared distance. Basis columns are
standardized on training data before ridge. Landmarks are uniform training
rows. Equal landmark indices/seeds and tuning grids are used across features.
Forecast horizons jointly select parameters by validation MSE. The three
branch target laws select independently. No refit incorporates validation
observations. Known bounded targets are clipped to[-1,1] for forecasts and
[-2.1,2.1] for branches. All feature/head combinations in pipelines() are
retained. Raw ridge, raw quadratic ridge and raw RBF are mandatory baselines.

The practical secondary validation selector chooses the lowest-validation-MSE
complete pipeline among raw ridge/RBF and ridge on KAHM or affine phi,
phi+logZ, local, and phi+raw (ten candidates). Its test score is evaluated
without further selection; selections and fitted states are saved.

Pilot, freeze, confirmation
There are two pilot repetitions, using a separate SeedSequence phase code.
The pilots check execution and set fixed practical effect margins:5% of the
mean pilot raw-ridge test MSE for each primary setting. No confirmation points
are generated before a dated SHA-256 freeze of code, config, analysis spec,
source manifest, data and margins. This is a recorded internal protocol
freeze, not a public prospective registration. All24 confirmation repetitions
independently regenerate training, validation and test data under new phase
seeds. Common randomness within a repetition supports paired comparisons.
Natural-data models are also run only after freeze.

Inference
The primary family contains27 cellwise contrasts (3 in each of9 settings):
  affine phi ridge MSE minus KAHM phi ridge MSE;
  KAHM phi ridge MSE minus KAHM phi+logZ ridge MSE;
  KAHM phi ridge MSE minus KAHM local ridge MSE.
Two additional contrasts compare the first advantage at lambda0 minus lambda1
at each curvature (train1024). All29 use two-sided paired Student t intervals
across24 complete repetitions with Bonferroni family alpha.05, df23. They are
parametric approximations, not the manuscript's distribution-free certificates.
Pointwise episode/row counts are not substituted for the24 training repetitions.
Nonsignificance is not equivalence. The fixed practical interaction margin is
the mean of its endpoint-cell margins. ANALYSIS_SPEC.txt supplies audit details.
Secondary models, smaller samples, horizon-specific results, diagnostics and
Airfoil remain exploratory/descriptive unless explicitly separately qualified.

Failure and audit policy
No failed repetition is silently removed or replaced. Errors are recorded.
An implementation correction after freeze requires an amendment, invalidation
of affected output and regeneration; no favorable-seed substitution. Saved
primary predictions, target/group arrays and per-unit losses support an
independent recomputation. Saved banks and selected primary heads support
replay. Runtime reflects shared computations and is not a fair standalone
end-to-end benchmarking comparison. Storage counts are qualified accordingly.

Interpretive limits
Equal feature counts do not imply equal capacity. Augmentations add information
and/or expressivity and dimensions. A finite RBF comparison cannot establish
Bayes risk or representation sufficiency. Better reconstruction from X does
not establish recovery from phi. Synthetic controlled evidence identifies
mechanisms only within these data laws; one natural dataset cannot establish
general forecasting superiority. Local coordinates and log-normalizer algebra
are established constructions; novelty would be the KAHM-specific controlled
attribution, supported scope and tested practical consequence.
