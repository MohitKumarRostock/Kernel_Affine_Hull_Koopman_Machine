\subsection{Notation and basic conventions}
\label{sec:notation}

Vectors are columns unless used as data-matrix rows. We write $\|\cdot\|_2$,
$\|\cdot\|_{\rm F}$, and $\|\cdot\|_{\rm op}$ for Euclidean, Frobenius,
and operator norms. Matrix inner products are
$\langle M,N\rangle_{\rm F}=\operatorname{tr}(M^\top N)$.
Logarithms are natural; $z_+=\max\{z,0\}$;
$\|V\|_{L^2}=(\mathbb E\|V\|_2^2)^{1/2}$.
The projection $\Pi_{\mathcal Y}$ onto a nonempty closed convex output
set is nonexpansive; for a box it clips coordinates. All random variables
and prediction maps are measurable.

Table~\ref{tab:notation} collects the main quantities. The forecasting
construction and the statistical statements below give their precise domains
and sampling assumptions.
