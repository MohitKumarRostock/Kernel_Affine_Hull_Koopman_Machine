# Data provenance and access

The fixed-geometry and target-changing studies generate synthetic data using the supplied source and fixed random-stream identifiers. Their data-generating equations, sample allocations and seed derivations are in [Experimental protocols](PROTOCOLS.md). They require no external dataset.

The atmospheric CO2 study uses the weekly Mauna Loa atmospheric carbon dioxide snapshot distributed by statsmodels 0.14.6 (`statsmodels/datasets/co2/co2.csv`). Source: C. D. Keeling and T. P. Whorf, *Atmospheric CO2 records from sites in the SIO air sampling network* (2004), Carbon Dioxide Information Analysis Center, Oak Ridge National Laboratory. See the [statsmodels dataset description](https://www.statsmodels.org/stable/datasets/generated/co2.html). The statsmodels dataset metadata identifies the data as public domain. The original CSV is included unchanged at [`real_data/co2.csv`](../real_data/co2.csv), SHA-256 `16695fa2786e53414e5a6b54767a3fdf5de99cfbc68617f69d1362d92776a92f`. Its fields are `date` (YYYYMMDD) and `co2` (ppm, missing values empty). No download or network access is necessary for regeneration. No external observations are added to the historical snapshot.

The earlier forecasting experiment retains the lossless compressed per-run loss data and sixteen accompanying numerical records in [`reproduction/score_enrichment/evidence/`](../reproduction/score_enrichment/evidence/). These are author-generated records; the complete original execution materials are unavailable. Its [archive manifest](../reproduction/score_enrichment/MANIFEST.json) identifies distributed records and their hashes.

The [capacity-comparison summary](../related_evidence/capacity_primary_contrasts.csv) preserves all twelve reported decoder-capacity comparisons. It is separate from the original 40-comparison saved-loss record and the executable experiments. The [supporting evidence reference](RELATED_EVIDENCE.md) identifies the limits of the other reported evaluations.

The [software license](../LICENSE) and [data license](../DATA_LICENSE) govern author-owned material. The public-domain CO2 observations retain their original status; derived experimental results are separate research outputs. The root `MANIFEST.json` identifies the distributed data, code and documentation by content digest. Recorded execution descriptions and checksums are provenance records; they do not by themselves establish when a protocol was fixed or validate its statistical assumptions.
