# Coefficient-budget sensitivity: design and numerical verification

This study was designed after the main experiment. It compares coefficient
budgets 1, 4, and 16 at history length 12, using new fitting seeds 2701, 2702,
and 2703. All other main-study settings are unchanged. Training, validation,
calibration, and test episodes use separate seed domains. Within each system
and seed, all three budgets share those data streams and geometric feature
blocks, providing a comparison with common random numbers.

The four systems and three seeds give twelve repetitions per budget. Each
repetition contains 21 candidates, for 36 completed runs and 756 fitted models.
The selected KAHM prefix within each budget minimizes validation MSE averaged
over the three horizons. Test error is not used to choose a budget.

Each budget-specific family receives failure probability `0.05/3`, including
its risk bounds and paired refinement comparisons. A union bound gives joint
confidence 0.95 across the three budget families within a system–seed
repetition. The confidence statement does not apply simultaneously across all
twelve system–seed repetitions.

## Records and aggregation

Scalar results and candidate descriptions are retained under
`results/sensitivity_v1_k1`, `results/sensitivity_v1_k4`, and
`results/sensitivity_v1_k16`. Full per-episode score archives are generated by
the fixed-seed runner and are excluded from Git. The archived audits record
their original checks; reprocessing those scores requires the full archives
or their regeneration in the corresponding source environment.

The derived files are `results/sensitivity_summary.json`,
`results/sensitivity_selected_results.csv`, and
`paper/tables/sensitivity_results.tex`. Table entries average each repetition's
RMSE, rather than taking the square root of an MSE averaged across repetitions.
The certification count concerns the validation-selected predictor at all
three horizons.

## Numerical checks

The archived formula and provenance audits report:

| Budget | Runs | Candidate–horizon certificates | Paired comparisons | Result |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 1 | 12 | 756 | 108 | Passed |
| 4 | 12 | 756 | 108 | Passed |
| 16 | 12 | 756 | 108 | Passed |

No model reported nonconvergence. Maximum recorded first-order optimality gaps
were `9.33572e-8`, `9.99774e-8`, and `9.97032e-8` for budgets 1, 4, and 16,
respectively. A separate scalar-record check reconstructed all table
entries and the confidence arithmetic from retained scalar records and
confirmed identical seed-domain records across budgets. It did not repeat the
model fits or independently recompute the archived per-episode moments.

The three budget campaigns ran concurrently with one BLAS/OpenMP thread per
process. Recorded campaign wall times were approximately 98, 101, and 103
seconds; these values depend on the execution environment.
