SCOPE AND EVIDENCE

The local-dimension specification distinguishes the initial cap from the
stored rank and from the number of retained features. Section 11.2 and
Supplement Section S9 report the completed dimension-policy study. The
previously revised theoretical development and earlier studies are preserved.
The new experiment compares fixed cap two, native cap twenty, and validation-
selected cap on 26 fitting repetitions before independent calibration.
Its 0.05 family allocation covers 4,680 designated one-sided statements.
Descriptive accuracy improves in 18 repetitions and ties in eight, but the
selected-forecast tolerance decisions do not change. Zero lower endpoints
do not establish zero population risk or class adequacy.

The current presentation uses the already completed and independently audited
study. No model fitting, new forecasts, or new experimental decisions were
performed during manuscript revision. The numerical audit's score-level scope
covers all 26 repetitions; full-array checks cover the six prespecified
repetition-zero jobs. README_BUILD.txt identifies the computational extension,
source freeze, separate numerical records, and exact limits of this evidence.
