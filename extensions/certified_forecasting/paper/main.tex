\begin{abstract}
We study lower bounds on the prediction risk achievable from a fixed
representation with a prescribed decoder sensitivity. A pairwise obstruction
is inexpensive to compute but can miss almost all of the optimal risk. For
scalar targets, we introduce a hierarchy of normalized minimum-risk problems
on blocks of observations. Its size-two member equals the pairwise witness;
its expectations increase to the optimal Lipschitz risk under boundedness and
compactness. Exact formulas characterize rare conflicts and noisy finite
supports, showing why no fixed block size is universally sufficient.
Independent-block calibration and stratification with estimated masses yield
finite-sample lower certificates. Combined with upper risk bounds, these can
certify the minimum feasible cost among declared representation classes.
Experiments distinguish population improvement from finite-sample uncertainty
and compare against full-sample empirical-minimum calibration. A controlled
nonlinear forecasting study tests these decisions with learned
Kernel Affine Hull Machine dictionaries and independent confirmation data.
Broader synthetic, pen-trajectory, and speech studies show task-dependent
accuracy and sensitivity to training-fitted input scaling, including scaling
to $[-1,1]$. The guarantees concern expected squared error under an explicit
sampling law; informative natural-data class exclusion remains limited.
\end{abstract}

\section*{Code, data, and research transparency}
The \href{https://github.com/MohitKumarRostock/Kernel_Affine_Hull_Koopman_Machine/tree/research/certified-soft-state-forecasting/extensions/certified_forecasting}{accompanying repository} provides the implementation, frozen configurations,
result summaries, and figure generators in
\path{extensions/certified_forecasting}; editable manuscript files are in its
\path{paper} directory. The forecasting, input-scaling, matched-geometry,
lower-bound comparison, block-hierarchy, learned-KAHM decision, controlled decision, speech, and speech-scaling studies
have separate
protocols and result directories. These provide configurations, source hashes,
per-episode scores, and outcomes for every prescribed configuration.
Appendix~\ref{app:protocols} specifies the forecasting configurations and
independent sampling units. The reproduction guide is
\path{extensions/certified_forecasting/docs/REPRODUCIBILITY.md}.
Public data are identified by source and checksum; download scripts preserve
attribution and license information. Appendix~\ref{app:speech-protocol} describes the speech study's evaluation
order and its implications for interpretation.
