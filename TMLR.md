The [feature-augmentation study](https://github.com/MohitKumarRostock/Kernel_Affine_Hull_Koopman_Machine/tree/paper/geometry-induced-soft-state-clean-2026-11-02) provides evidence for **Feature Augmentation for Forecasting with Reconstruction-Based Representations**. Its [reproduction guide](https://github.com/MohitKumarRostock/Kernel_Affine_Hull_Koopman_Machine/blob/paper/geometry-induced-soft-state-clean-2026-11-02/REPRODUCIBILITY.md) covers the statistical analysis of saved per-run losses and the separate predictor-capacity experiment. The [risk-bound study](https://github.com/MohitKumarRostock/Kernel_Affine_Hull_Koopman_Machine/tree/research/risk-certificates-consolidated-2026-11-08) provides evidence for **A Hierarchy of Lower Bounds on Prediction Risk for Forecasting Representations**, which asks whether any predictor in a specified class can meet a prediction-error requirement.

## Four-arm feature-ablation experiment

**Geometry-Induced Soft-State Abstractions for Forecasting: Predictive Enrichment with Kernel Affine Hull Machines**

The [study files](https://github.com/MohitKumarRostock/Kernel_Affine_Hull_Koopman_Machine/tree/6c39a003e61ace058851ec6badca3e8dd13ef183/) contain the article, source files and numerical records for a four-arm ablation with 32 repetitions. It uses a different protocol from the 64-repetition feature-augmentation experiment.

The four-arm study completed 32 independent repetitions and 256 tasks. Three of four primary criteria are established: local position and KAHM enrichment improve their designated nonlinear references by 69.201% and 10.459%, and linear noninferiority passes. KAHM's 0.0292% nonlinear gain over matched RBF does not establish the prescribed 3% margin. For the linear-predictor comparison, loss is 1.111% higher than its prespecified reference, within the 5% noninferiority tolerance. Selecting each procedure’s maximum local dimension separately on validation data is a secondary analysis; the comparisons do not establish autonomous state dynamics.

The scientific source is [commit `2b7d19b5`](https://github.com/MohitKumarRostock/Kernel_Affine_Hull_Koopman_Machine/commit/2b7d19b5b47f46b5b21b28d24960688f7969d96f), published before production. The completed independent numerical audit covers all 256 tasks; it is not external scientific replication.
