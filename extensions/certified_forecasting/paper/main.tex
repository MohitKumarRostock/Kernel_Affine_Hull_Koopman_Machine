\begin{abstract}%
Kernel Affine Hull Machines (KAHMs) describe data through local geometric
reconstruction models. We develop a forecasting procedure that turns their soft
associations into predictions of the observed future signal and evaluates when
those predictions are adequate. The learner retains KAHM dictionary blocks and
fits constrained multihorizon decoders. A sum of block coefficient norms
preserves earlier prediction classes and prevents exact duplication from
enlarging the feasible class. A pairwise discrepancy between target and
representation separation lower-bounds the risk of every decoder with a
prescribed Lipschitz budget; it is exact on a two-atom law and dominates a
root-variance obstruction under the same conditional pairing law.
Independent-episode calibration combines this lower certificate with
fitted-risk upper bounds, paired refinement comparisons, and explicit
abstention. The theory also applies to other fixed representations.
Experiments cover exact-risk calibration, six synthetic dynamics, and
writer-disjoint pen trajectories. Matched RBF and affine-hull controls identify
KAHM benefits on nonlinear autoregression and pen trajectories, alongside
substantial affine-hull advantages on the oscillators. Input-scaling ablations
retain replicated gains and regressions. Few independent evaluation units can
leave certificates uninformative. The guarantees concern expected squared
error under a specified episode law.
\end{abstract}
\begin{keywords}
Kernel Affine Hull Machines, time-series forecasting, soft state abstraction,
representation refinement, prediction risk certification
\end{keywords}

\section*{Reproducibility and research provenance}
The implementation, frozen configurations, retained result summaries, and
figure generators are distributed in the
\path{extensions/certified_forecasting} directory of the accompanying
repository. Editable manuscript source is included in its \path{paper}
directory. The original experiments, input-scaling follow-up, and matched
geometry/head comparison are retained separately, with frozen configurations,
source hashes, and documented exact replays of damaged score archives in the
scaling study. The evidence packages identify the retained score arrays;
fixed-seed runners regenerate full per-episode archives. The experimental
configurations and independent sampling units are specified in the appendices. No external journal submission is represented by
this draft. AI assistance was used for derivation exploration, implementation,
literature retrieval, and writing. Independent author verification of the
scientific claims, authorship information, and submission declarations is still
required; funding and conflict-of-interest declarations must be supplied by the
authors before submission.
