# Completed KAHM time-series research: evidence and interpretation

The final reconstruction-mechanism experiment has been executed. Its fixed decision is **STOP_FORECASTING_PERFORMANCE_BRANCH**. The completed result supports research on the conditions connecting geometric reconstruction, decoder access, stability and predictive utility; it does not support expanding this tested KAHM branch through additional forecasting benchmarks or another adaptive variant.

## Three distinct findings

1. **Basis distinctness is conditional.** Direct normalized KAHM target transfer is exactly a normalized kernel-ridge ratio. Symmetric banks can produce the same raw augmented span as matched RBF. An irregular three-reference construction proves that distinct raw KAHM basis functions also exist. The reconstruction, feature transformation and decoder must be evaluated together.
2. **Correction magnitude can be controlled.** Separate radial feature bounds and correctly scaled centered ridge give a uniform correction bound. All declared empirical stability checks pass. This does not bound the raw baseline, guarantee prediction accuracy, or establish stability of online geometry refreshes.
3. **The practical KAHM-specific advantage is not established.** On the complete fixed experiment, KAHM improves the common basis by 12.58%, but improves matched RBF by only 1.17%. Its 3.92% raw-RBF point gain has an unresolved simultaneous 3%-margin lower bound. Boosted trees and the C-selected non-KAHM policy have lower aggregate errors. Only the common-basis margin and linear-control noninferiority pass among six required conditions.

The positive common-basis result matters: the experiment does not show that reconstruction-derived features are useless. It demonstrates why the existence of a distinct function and a bounded readout are insufficient to establish an advantageous KAHM forecasting method.

## Boundaries of the conclusion

The three nonlinear laws were known during development; fresh seeds provide independent realizations, not new applications. The fixed margins are research-allocation thresholds, and the six one-sided paired-t bounds are approximate under their stated assumptions. The inferential sample is 24 repetition aggregates, not millions of overlapping forecast values. The local execution freeze is not claimed as public preregistration. The internal independent-code audit is not external replication.

The fixed-budget failure is a research decision for these procedures and conditions. It neither proves that every geometric representation fails nor adds a new universal impossibility certificate. Formal exclusion of a target accuracy requires a stated representation, decoder class, norm/sensitivity restriction and evaluation law. Existing related prediction-limit work already addresses such declared classes; the current result does not replace those assumptions.
