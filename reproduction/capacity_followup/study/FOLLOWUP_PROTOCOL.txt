CAPACITY FOLLOW-UP, FROZEN AFTER STAGE1 AND BEFORE NEW STAGE2 DATA

Reason for follow-up
The first-stage secondary models show a promising KAHM-local+RBF result on
Van der Pol (mean MSE0.001049 versus0.001405 affine-local and0.002978 rawRBF),
while the same comparison does not favor KAHM on Duffing. A concrete remaining
question is whether this is conditional on the64-landmark approximation budget.
This follow-up is explicitly motivated by those observed results. It is not
part of the first-stage29-comparison family or an original pre-data hypothesis.

Design frozen before generating any follow-up data
24 independent new repetitions of all three forecasting laws: nonlinear AR,
Van der Pol and Duffing. Identical history, horizon, episodes, preprocessing,
geometry-bank and tuning specifications to stage1. New SeedSequence phase4:
[20261030,4,replicate,100+system_index,stream], with streams0/1/2 for splits,
10 for geometry and11 for head landmarks. The same new splits and fitted bank
are used by all competing models within a repetition.

Features: raw; matched affine phi plus gated local PCA coordinates; matched
KAHM phi plus gated local PCA coordinates; and KAHM full folding scores.
Decoder: the exact existing standardized Nystrom-RBF plus linear diagnostic,
with64 and256 training landmarks. The same training row-selection seed and
validation tuning grids are used. Changing width also changes the sampled
landmark set; sets need not be nested. This is an approximate decoder budget
comparison, not a bound on the optimal raw-input prediction rule.

Primary comparisons
At each of3systems and2widths:
  raw RBF MSE minus KAHM-local RBF MSE;
  affine-local RBF MSE minus KAHM-local RBF MSE.
This gives12 two-sided Bonferroni-adjusted paired t intervals across24 fresh
repetitions (df23), family alpha.05. Five percent of the corresponding
stage1 mean rawRBF MSE is frozen as the absolute practical margin for both
widths. These thresholds use previous-stage data, never stage2 outcomes.

KAHM-score models and horizon-specific values are secondary. Contrasting
significance across widths does not itself prove an interaction; direct width
response patterns are descriptive unless an interaction is separately tested.
One secondary operational selector chooses the lowest-validation-MSE pipeline
among all eight complete feature-by-width candidates, after each candidate's
own validation tuning. It uses no test results. Its predictions, selected
candidate and per-episode test losses are saved. This selector was specified
before generating any stage2 data; it is not a member of the12 primary family.
No claim of joint95% family coverage across both study stages is made. Both
families rely on t approximations rather than distribution-free certificates.

Integrity and scope
followup_freeze.json records all new code/specification hashes and the original
protocol-freeze hash. Both freeze sets are verified before execution. No stage1
frozen file is changed. Every failed repetition is recorded and retained;
implementation corrections require a new amendment and affected regeneration.
Saved predictions, groups, targets and per-episode losses permit independent
loss recomputation. Complete model sources, chosen parameters, seeds and fitted
geometry banks permit reruns. Large fitted RBF states are not archived.

The follow-up addresses finite decoder capacity as a plausible alternative
explanation. It still concerns fixed synthetic laws, one small geometric-bank
specification and two finite feature budgets. It does not establish superiority
over fully tuned modern baselines, the Bayes rule, or all possible raw decoders.
