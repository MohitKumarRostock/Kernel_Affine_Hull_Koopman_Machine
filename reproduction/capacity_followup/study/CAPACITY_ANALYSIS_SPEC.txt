KAHM GEOMETRY STUDY: FRESH DECODER-CAPACITY FOLLOW-UP
Version 1.1, to be frozen before phase-4 observations are generated

MOTIVATION AND RELATION TO THE FIRST STAGE
This is a separately planned, data-motivated follow-up. It was proposed after
inspecting the completed first-stage secondary RBF-head results. It is not
part of the first stage's original 29-comparison confirmation family.

The question is whether the performance of KAHM local-coordinate features
relative to raw histories and matched affine local-coordinate features
persists with a larger finite Nyström landmark budget. A favorable result
with 64 landmarks alone does not establish representation superiority or an
advantage over a well-tuned unrestricted kernel method.

All choices below, the executable follow-up configuration, source hashes,
and numerical practical-effect margins must be frozen before generating any
phase-4 observation. The first-stage outputs may motivate these choices, but
the new confirmation observations must be independent of all first-stage
training, validation, and test observations. No first-stage row is reused as
a new confirmation unit.

1. DATA, FEATURES, AND DECODERS

Use phase='capacity_confirmation', RNG phase code 4, master seed 20261030,
and 24 complete independent replicates, IDs 0,...,23. The three systems are
nonlinear_ar, Van der Pol, and Duffing, with setting IDs matching stage 1:
forecast_nonlinear_ar, forecast_van_der_pol, and forecast_duffing.

Each system and replicate uses 128 independent training episodes, 128
independent validation episodes, and 512 independent test episodes. Histories,
horizons, origins, dynamical laws, noise laws, and bounds are unchanged from
the frozen first-stage configuration. Thirteen origins per episode provide
1664 training rows, not 1664 independent episodes. Equal-weight averaging
over horizons and origins within each episode precedes equal-weight averaging
over test episodes.

Fit one fresh unsupervised geometric bank per system and replicate, using
training inputs only. Reuse exactly that bank and the same train/validation/
test observations for all four encodings and both landmark budgets:
  raw; affine:local; kahm:local; kahm:scores.

The local encodings are the existing soft affinities augmented with
affinity-gated PCA coordinates of raw histories about their local reference
centers. They are not the full KAHM reconstructed vectors. KAHM and matched
affine conditions share the scaler, group partition, references, PCA bases,
and score formula, as in the first-stage controlled geometry experiment.

The head is the existing finite Nyström RBF head with a retained linear
component. Test n_landmarks in {64,256}, retaining the same prespecified
ridge-penalty and bandwidth-multiplier grids and validation selection rule.
Training data alone determine feature standardization, landmarks, bandwidth
heuristics, and all head coefficients. Validation chooses the final ridge
penalty and bandwidth. Test loss must not choose them.

Changing the landmark budget can also change the sampled landmarks and the
training-derived median bandwidth. This study compares these two declared
finite-head pipelines; it does not isolate a mathematical width parameter
while holding every other realized basis value fixed. Report actual retained
head dimensions, landmark counts, timing, and memory context. A 256-landmark
head is still not a universal-decoder oracle or an exhaustive tuning frontier.

A secondary operational selector chooses one complete pipeline among the
eight feature-by-landmark-budget candidates by the mean validation loss
alone, with ties resolved in the fixed executable pipeline order. The
selected pipeline is evaluated on the same untouched test episodes. This
selection rule is fixed before phase-4 data generation. Its outputs are
descriptive secondary outcomes, not additional primary contrasts. They are
stored as feature='validation_selector', head='selected', landmarks=0, with
the actual selected feature, landmark budget, and hyperparameters recorded.

2. PRIMARY FAMILY: TWELVE CONTRASTS

For each system s and landmark budget M in {64,256}, and every replicate r,
define paired improvements:

  D_raw(s,M,r) = MSE(raw,RBF_M) - MSE(kahm:local,RBF_M),
  D_affine(s,M,r) = MSE(affine:local,RBF_M)
                    - MSE(kahm:local,RBF_M).

Positive values favor KAHM local-coordinate features. There are exactly
3 systems * 2 budgets * 2 contrasts = 12 primary comparisons. All unfavorable
and inconclusive results remain in the primary table. kahm:scores is a
prespecified secondary diagnostic.

The primary family tests benefits separately at each budget. A significant
benefit at one budget and an inconclusive benefit at the other does not by
itself prove that benefits differ between budgets. Differences across budgets
may be described using point estimates and paired descriptive diagnostics;
there is no primary width-interaction hypothesis in this twelve-comparison
family. A benefit at 256 landmarks means it survives that tested finite-head
budget, not every stronger decoder.

3. PRACTICAL-EFFECT MARGINS

For each system, define a fixed margin using the complete 24 first-stage
confirmation raw-input RBF-head results:

  delta_s = 0.05 * mean(first-stage raw/RBF test MSE for system s).

Use the same delta_s for both landmark budgets and both contrasts. Preserve
all 24 contributing MSEs, their record hashes, the immutable first-stage
record-file hash, and the resulting unrounded margin in capacity_margins.json.
These are data-motivated study-design margins fixed before new confirmation;
they are not externally specified application tolerances. Conditional on the
completed first-stage data, the margins are fixed for the new experiment.

4. UNCERTAINTY AND CLAIMS

For each primary contrast, compute its mean Dbar and unbiased standard
deviation s_D across exactly 24 complete independent replicate differences.
The reported interval is

  Dbar +/- t_(23, 1 - 0.05/(2*12)) * s_D/sqrt(24).

These are two-sided Bonferroni paired-replicate Student t intervals. The
intended simultaneous confidence is 95% within this twelve-comparison family
under the paired-normal model; with nonnormal differences it is an
approximation. It is not a distribution-free risk certificate. Report
replicate variability and retain influential outcomes.

This new 95% family and the first stage's 95% family do NOT jointly constitute
one 95% simultaneous event over all 41 comparisons. Do not make that claim.
The follow-up's inferential interpretation is conditional on the completed
first stage and the frozen follow-up choices, with fresh independent data.

Use the fixed margin to interpret the same simultaneous interval:
  lower endpoint > delta_s: practical benefit under the declared threshold;
  lower endpoint > 0 only: positive effect, practical size unresolved;
  upper endpoint < 0: negative effect under the declared comparison;
  otherwise: direction unresolved.

An interval entirely contained in [-delta_s,+delta_s] supports a mean effect
within that declared band under the interval assumptions. Nonsignificance
alone does not establish equivalence. Report absolute MSEs as well as paired
differences. Relative reductions are descriptive unless their uncertainty is
separately derived.

5. INDEPENDENCE, FAILURES, AND AUDIT

The unit for the paired t intervals is the complete independently regenerated
train/validation/test replicate. Sharing data across features and budgets
within one replicate is deliberate pairing. Dependent origins and horizons
do not increase the number of independent replicates.

Run all 24 prescribed replicate IDs and all systems, features, and budgets.
Retain failures and do not replace poor-performing seeds. An incomplete
primary pair receives no nominal 24-replicate interval. Technical reruns must
use the exact frozen inputs/configuration and preserve previous failure logs.
Any substantive change after observing follow-up results is separately
identified and requires fresh data for a further confirmatory claim.

Archive row-level method summaries, selected parameters, predictions, test
targets, episode identifiers, and episode loss arrays. An independent
analyzer recomputes all twelve paired intervals and can reconstruct episode
losses directly from archived predictions and targets. Frozen source hashes
and stage-1 margin provenance are independently checked. No source file from
the first-stage frozen experiment is changed by this follow-up.

There are 576 RBF-grid outcome rows and 72 secondary-selector rows, for a
total of 648. The version-1.1 selector addition and the corresponding analyzer
revision were made before the full follow-up protocol freeze and before any
phase-4 data, after capacity_margins.json had frozen its numerical margins.
That numerical-margin file is preserved unchanged; the final follow-up freeze
pins these revised analysis files separately.
