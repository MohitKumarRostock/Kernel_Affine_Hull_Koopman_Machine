\section{Numerical diagnostics, costs, and verification}\label{sup:audit}
The completed cohort contains 7,680 local-model/cap records. All have positive rank; no rank-zero wrapper or projected-range reduction was used. Native reference constructions were compared against separate calls to the pinned autoencoder on the same reference arrays. The maximum recorded differences in bases, projected references, regularized inverses, and square-root metrics were zero. These are parity checks using shared primitives, not independent implementations of native PCA or KAHM.

The stack of local basis transposes has numerical column rank 12 in 254 of 256 cap-two banks and all cap-four, cap-eight, and cap-twelve banks. No cap-one stack has full column rank. The maximum recorded projected-metric condition number is approximately $2.05\times10^5$. The smallest observed memberships are $1.90\times10^{-44}$ for KAHM, $7.53\times10^{-46}$ for positive-kernel reconstruction, and $3.35\times10^{-43}$ for affine projection. These sampled quantities are not uniform bounds over an unobserved input domain.

The largest membership partition-sum discrepancy is $3.55\times10^{-15}$, and the largest numerical error in recovering scores from memberships and normalizers is $2.78\times10^{-16}$. The minimum observed signed relative denominator is $1.88\times10^{-5}$. There were no KAHM or positive-kernel query fallbacks. Nevertheless, 309,327 repeated local-model/query evaluations would have had a completely zero kernel row without log-kernel rescaling. The counts refer to evaluations across models, caps, and splits, not independent observations. Constructed tests separately cover cancellation, negative denominators, rank-zero data, and affine fallback with recomputed scores.

\subsection{Computational audit}
The released audit covers 256 tasks, 843,264 candidate ridge-head refits, 62,464 C/D forecast-array replays, 688,128 reconstructed windows, and 26,624 selected loss records. All 40 primary intervals and decisions were independently recomputed from the saved losses. The largest absolute discrepancy in B forecast calculations is $3.84\times10^{-8}$ under the componentwise tolerances stated in Section~\ref{sup:method}. The maximum recorded discrepancies in C/D replay and selected-loss reconstruction are zero. A nonzero absolute discrepancy can be acceptable when it satisfies the combined relative and absolute tolerance at that element.

Production used one checked SVD recovery. The independent audit used its prespecified augmented-QR calculation for 248 candidate heads. Neither changes the predictor or its tuning menu. All candidates and tasks were retained. The original complete audit, source manifest, stage identities, and final release receipt are provided; the audit was not inferred from successful document compilation.

The audit shares the feature encoder and native routines. It reconstructs windows from saved episodes and uses distinct ridge calculations, but it does not independently reimplement the physical integrators or partition fitting. It is internal computational verification, not replication by an independent group. Re-executing the recorded seeds would provide computational reproduction, not additional independent repetitions.
