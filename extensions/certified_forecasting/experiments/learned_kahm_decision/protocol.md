# Learned KAHM representation decisions

This is a new controlled mechanism experiment. Five development-only pilot seeds compared a discontinuous sign-product target and a smooth product target. The final protocol uses the smooth bounded product and records that adaptation openly. No pilot calibration stream was generated. The twenty confirmatory repetitions in each of two regimes use disjoint random streams and new training samples. No naturally acquired data are used.

A history has two independently sampled signed amplitudes, each magnitude uniform on [0.8,1]. The repairable one-step target is `0.8*x1*x2 + uniform(-0.05,0.05)`, a smooth nonlinear two-lag recurrence at the fixed forecast origin. In the irreducible control, the product is formed from a new independent hidden history. Targets lie in [-0.85,0.85]. The declared MSE tolerance is 0.10 in these fixed target units. It is a study-design tolerance, not an externally mandated application requirement.

Each trial fits the repository's actual KAHM implementation with two and four clusters. Training-fitted coordinate min-max scaling precedes geometry fitting and is frozen for every later query; later values are not clipped. The second class retains both blocks, so its coordinate cost is six, compared with two for the first class. Both heads obey a common sum-of-block-norms budget of two. Three ridge penalties are compared on validation separately in each class. All forty banks and eighty selected decoders are frozen together before any calibration sample is generated. The frozen maps also define two history-only partitions by the argmax of their two- and four-cluster association blocks. Neither oracle quadrant labels nor future outcomes define those partitions.

Each trial uses independent training (2048), validation (1024), and calibration (16384) episodes. Calibration endpoints are reported at all four prescribed prefixes: 256,1024,4096,16384. Endpoints cover fitted-risk upper bounds, unconditional class lower bounds, and both learned-partition lower bounds with estimated stratum masses. The total failure allocation is 0.05 jointly across both regimes, all forty trials, all four sizes, both classes, and every reported bound. Nested sample prefixes do not require independence because their individual failure probabilities are union-bounded.

An upper endpoint can accept the six-coordinate fitted forecast. Excluding the two-coordinate Lipschitz class as well certifies six as the minimum feasible cost among these two declared KAHM classes. Excluding both classes certifies their joint infeasibility. Neither statement ranks KAHM against all forecasting methods or establishes a population frequency of useful decisions on natural data.

The JSON protocol is authoritative for seeds, hyperparameters, confidence allocations and reporting. The runner has separate `develop` and `calibrate` stages, hashes all selected models and execution sources before calibration, refuses overwrite, and retains all outcomes. Large generated NPZ arrays are reproducible and are not committed. Portable compressed JSON contains the complete frozen numerical KAHM banks and selected coefficients, so verification can reconstruct every calibration feature, prediction and score without refitting a model.
