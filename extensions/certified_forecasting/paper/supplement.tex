\begin{abstract}
This supplement gives the local KAHM reconstruction procedure and the
experimental protocols supporting the main manuscript. It includes
finite-support lower-bound comparisons, the explicit-feature decision control,
full forecasting tables, preprocessing comparisons, matched geometries and
decoders, speech-energy evidence, and a separate study of local dimension
selection. The dimension study reports all validation-selected forecasts,
retained-prefix certificates, and planned fitted-improvement comparisons
under one simultaneous confidence allocation. Adverse and inconclusive
outcomes are included. All proofs of the main paper's stated results remain
in the main paper. The research sources supply frozen configurations,
individual outcomes, source hashes, and reproduction commands for each
separately defined study.
\end{abstract}

\paragraph{Reading conventions.}
Sections, tables, figures, and equations in this supplement have an S prefix.
References without that prefix refer to the main paper. The reference studies
and the dimension-policy study have separate designs and confidence
allocations. Repeated fitting
on a previously examined natural-data cohort is descriptive; it does not
create an independent confirmation sample.
