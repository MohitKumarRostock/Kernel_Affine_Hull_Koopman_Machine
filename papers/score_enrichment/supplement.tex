\begin{abstract}
This supplement supports an investigation of whether reconstruction-score abstractions retain predictive distinctions and make them accessible to finite decoders. It specifies the numerical observation laws, representations, decoder construction, validation selection, and statistical analysis of the matched forecasting study. It reports every primary confidence interval and the terminal means and repetition standard deviations of all procedures under both selection rules. Numerical diagnostics distinguish mathematical information recovery from finite-precision behavior. Separate controlled and policy studies are described with their original populations and comparison families, including unfavorable findings. The reproducibility specification distinguishes document compilation, saved-loss analysis, fitted-model replay, and simulation regeneration.
\end{abstract}
