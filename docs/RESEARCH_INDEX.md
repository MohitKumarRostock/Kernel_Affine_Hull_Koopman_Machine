# Current publication and historical evidence

## Current manuscript

[Predictive Enrichment of Geometry-Induced Soft-State Abstractions](../papers/score_enrichment/) studies information retention and finite-decoder access in reconstruction-score representations. The [current study directory](../extensions/score_enrichment_20261102/) contains the exact published primary-summary evidence and source identities. Its 64-repetition-per-law cohort and 40-comparison family remain separate from earlier studies.

## Immutable historical archive

Earlier manuscript editions, experiments, outputs, and original dependency trees are retained at [commit 5123365917ae630b684f8a3bebb0294ee695ce7a](https://github.com/MohitKumarRostock/Kernel_Affine_Hull_Koopman_Machine/tree/5123365917ae630b684f8a3bebb0294ee695ce7a). Branch `archive/pre-manuscript-cleanup-2026-11-02` provides a convenient entry point; the commit, not the moving branch name, defines the archival identity. No Git history or numerical result has been rewritten.

The current checkout contains only the current publication and its published evidence subset. The archive is not another copy of the current paper to choose between. It preserves the scientific history and all evidence, including unfavorable and unresolved results, outside the main reading route.

Reproducing those studies requires their archived checkout, rather than copying isolated files into the current paper tree.

The [reproduction guide](../REPRODUCIBILITY.md) distinguishes historical recovery from availability of the current full experimental package. Archiving earlier work neither supplies missing current arrays nor changes their availability status.
