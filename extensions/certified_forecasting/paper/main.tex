\begin{abstract}
We study whether a fixed representation can support a prescribed forecasting
accuracy and whether a more costly representation is necessary. Our approach
combines Kernel Affine Hull Machine (KAHM) features with decoders whose
sensitivity is bounded. A pairwise lower bound detects target separations that
exceed the permitted separation between predictions. We characterize when this
bound is exact and construct distributions for which it is arbitrarily loose.
Pairing within predefined strata can improve the bound while preserving the
population risk being evaluated. Finite-sample certificates account for
estimated stratum probabilities and combine with upper risk bounds to accept
fitted forecasts, exclude decoder classes, or certify minimum feasible
representation cost. Appending feature blocks under a sum-of-block-norms budget
preserves earlier predictors without enlarging the class when a block is
duplicated. Experiments compare the pairwise certificate with variance-based
bounds and bounds obtained by minimizing empirical risk; the latter are often
tighter. A controlled forecasting experiment demonstrates cost and infeasibility
decisions, although substantial calibration samples are required. Applications
include six synthetic systems, pen trajectories, and speech energy. KAHM
performance depends on the task and decoder. All guarantees concern expected
squared error under an explicit sampling law.
\end{abstract}

\section*{Code, data, and research transparency}
The \href{https://github.com/MohitKumarRostock/Kernel_Affine_Hull_Koopman_Machine/tree/research/certified-soft-state-forecasting/extensions/certified_forecasting}{accompanying repository} provides the implementation, frozen configurations,
result summaries, and figure generators in
\path{extensions/certified_forecasting}; editable manuscript files are in its
\path{paper} directory. The forecasting, input-scaling, matched-geometry,
lower-bound comparison, controlled decision, and speech studies have separate
protocols and result directories. These provide configurations, source hashes,
per-episode scores, and outcomes for every prescribed configuration.
Appendix~\ref{app:protocols} specifies the forecasting configurations and
independent sampling units. The reproduction guide is
\path{extensions/certified_forecasting/docs/REPRODUCIBILITY.md}.
Public data are identified by source and checksum; download scripts preserve
attribution and license information. Section~\ref{sec:speech} describes the
speech study's evaluation order and its implications for interpretation.
