\begin{abstract}
This supplement specifies the observation laws, numerical construction, readout selection, and complete results for the matched comparison of membership, local-position, KAHM, and positive-kernel RBF features. It reports all primary comparisons and the descriptive results by law, noise level, horizon, and dimension-selection rule. A self-contained ridge calculation states the limited implication of bounded feature norms. Separate residual and policy evaluations are reported with their original comparison families, including unsuccessful superiority criteria and an unfavorable descriptive natural-series result. The reproducibility specification distinguishes recomputation from retained summaries, replay of fitted models, and regeneration of the simulation cohort.
\end{abstract}
